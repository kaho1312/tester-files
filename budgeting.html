Tester instructions: Budgeting spreadsheets

Thanks for testing! This is a pre-release digital product. Please use made-up numbers only - never enter your real financial or personal details.
Time: about 15 minutes total (about 8-10 min with the file, then the survey).

Everything opens in your web browser. You do not need to download or install anything.

How to open

  • Spreadsheets: click the link. Google shows a “Make a copy” screen – click Make a copy. Your own copy opens in Google Sheets in your browser, ready to type in. (You need to be signed in to a Google account; a free one is fine.) Nothing to download or install.

What to do

You will be assigned ONE or TWO of these files in the survey (follow the survey):

  1. Open Paycheck-to-Paycheck Budget (Google Sheet) – click “Make a copy”
    Read the "Instructions" tab, then on "Budget": enter one paycheck amount and 3 bills (e.g. Rent 900, Phone 45, Car 210). Find and write down the "leftover" amount.
  2. Open Debt Snowball Tracker (Google Sheet) – click “Make a copy”
    On "Debts", replace the example debts with 3 made-up debts (balance, rate, minimum payment) and an extra monthly payment. Find the debt-free date and compare snowball vs avalanche. Log one payment on "Payment Log".
  3. Open Multi-Currency Expat Budget (Google Sheet) – click “Make a copy”
    Set an exchange rate, then add 3 transactions in different currencies on "Transactions". Check the "Summary" tab: what is the total spent in your home currency?

When done

Go back to the survey and answer the questions. Please tell us which device and browser you used. Note anything confusing, broken, misspelled or ugly - honest criticism is the most useful thing you can give.
