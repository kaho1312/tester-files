Tester instructions: Fitness guide + lifting tracker

Thanks for testing! This is a pre-release digital product. Please use made-up numbers only - never enter your real financial or personal details.
Time: about 15 minutes total (about 8-10 min with the file, then the survey).

Everything opens in your web browser. You do not need to download or install anything.

How to open

  • Guides (PDF): click the link and the guide opens right in your browser. Nothing to download. Printing one page is optional.
  • Spreadsheets: click the link. Google shows a “Make a copy” screen – click Make a copy. Your own copy opens in Google Sheets in your browser, ready to type in. (You need to be signed in to a Google account; a free one is fine.) Nothing to download or install.

What to do

  1. Open the Bodyweight 30-Day Challenge (PDF) in your browser
    Skim the guide (2-3 min). Find what Day 1 asks you to do and how to make a workout easier or harder. Would you know what to do tomorrow with no equipment?
  2. Open Lifting Tracker (Google Sheet) – click “Make a copy”
    On "Log", enter 3 sets of any exercise (e.g. Push-up 3 x 15, or Bench 3 x 8 @ 60 kg). Check "Exercises & PRs" for your estimated 1-rep max, and look at the "Progress" charts.

When done

Go back to the survey and answer the questions. Please tell us which device and browser you used. Note anything confusing, broken, misspelled or ugly - honest criticism is the most useful thing you can give.
